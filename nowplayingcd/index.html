💿 Now Playing CD

What you're listening to, on a CD player from back then. · Android

Play a song in YouTube Music or any music app, and Now Playing CD shows it on a 90s CD player. A CD printed with the album cover spins, a new CD goes in when the album changes, and the remote controls your music app. Open it and the music app you last used picks up where you left off.

Now Playing CD doesn't play music itself. Your music app keeps playing as usual — Now Playing CD shows it and controls it.

Get it on Google Play

1. First launch — connect your music app

  1. Open Now Playing CD → when "Connect your music app" appears, tap Open settings.
  2. Turn on Now Playing CD under notification access → Allow.
  3. Go back to Now Playing CD. That's it.

Why notification access? On Android it's the only way to see what another app is playing (title, cover, position). Now Playing CD reads only playback info — never the contents of your notifications.

2. Playing music

  • Play a song in your music app and come back — the CD goes in and starts spinning.
  • A new CD goes in when the album changes. Next track on the same album? The CD keeps spinning.
  • Cover art comes from your music app first; if there's none, it's looked up by album name. If nothing is found, you get a rainbow CD.
  • No need to open your music app. When you open Now Playing CD, "▶ Resuming in …" appears and the app you last listened to starts playing (turn off in Menu → Play on open). Tap ▶ on the remote any time to do the same. To use another app, pick it in Menu → Music apps.

3. Skins

Tap Skin (bottom right) to choose a player: Classroom (a slot-in player on a desk) or Bus (a portable player whose lid opens and closes).

6. Screen, lock & quit

  • The screen stays on while the app is open — nice on a desk. It also shows right on the lock screen.
  • Phones stay in portrait; unfolded foldables and tablets can rotate.
  • Lock blocks accidental touches. Press and hold anywhere for a second to unlock.
  • Quit pauses the music and closes the app.

7. Troubleshooting

No song shows up. Check that Now Playing CD is on under notification access and that your music app is actually playing. App updates sometimes turn the access off — the prompt will appear again; tap Open settings.

▶ doesn't start a music app I've never used. Play a song in that app once (OPEN opens it); after that ▶ works.

The alarm doesn't ring. Make sure Alarms & reminders is allowed for Now Playing CD in Settings.
