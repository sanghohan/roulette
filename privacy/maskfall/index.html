Effective: 2026-10-10 · Android game MASKFALL (net.donjiral.quantumfighter)

MASKFALL ("the game") sends no personal information to the developer. It has no sign-up, no ads and no analytics. This section explains what the game handles on your device and when it talks to Google services.
